Who We Are

We are a bilingual data consulting agency delivering accurate, structured, and scalable data services for machine learning, research, and enterprise analytics. Our consultants are specialized professionals, certified in their domain of expertise by advanced diplomas and globally recognized certification. All our consultants are bilingual or multilingual (mainly English and French, but also Spanish and German if needed). We understand your data!

What We Do

  • Data annotation and quality assurance
  • Multilingual dataset creation and curation
  • Data infrastructure advisory
  • Human-in-the-loop data workflows
  • Custom analytics solutions

Our technical expertise includes every step of the data chain, from the storage to the final visualizations:

  • SQL databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL/SQuirreL)
  • data coding: Python & R
  • ETL set ups: Azure Data Factory, Apache, Oracle, Hadoop, Microsoft SQL Server
  • Solution Engineering (specialized Azure Cloud solutions)
  • Data visualization: Power BI & Tableau
